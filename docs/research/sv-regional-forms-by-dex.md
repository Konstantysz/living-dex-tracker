# SV regional and alternate forms by dex

Scope: Scarlet/Violet with The Teal Mask (Kitakami) and The Indigo Disk (Blueberry).
Method: Serebii species pages, fetched through a summarizer (so wording is second-hand). Bulbapedia was not fetched. Many species were not checked. They are marked "unverified".
Base URL pattern: https://www.serebii.net/pokedex-sv/SPECIES/

## Open conflicts (summary)

1. Johto Wooper: Serebii says "trade in Cascarrafa" for Paldea. Serebii also lists wild Wooper in Kitakami but does not name the form. Web search says Johto Wooper is wild in Kitakami AND an NPC trade in Cascarrafa. Likely both are true. The Kitakami claim is single-source.
2. Kanto Tauros: Serebii says it comes from breeding Paldean Tauros in Kitakami. The Blueberry dex has a Tauros entry with no form named. The Savanna fixed encounter has no breed named. Blueberry form is unverified.
3. Alolan/Galarian/Hisuian forms in Blueberry: Serebii's Blueberry dex page does not name forms. Only image filenames suggest them (-a, -g, -h). The "Terarium" source is not stated on the pages read.
4. Vulpix/Ninetales (Scarlet) and Sandshrew/Sandslash (Violet): Serebii confirms the Blueberry exclusivity and the trade route. It does not say which FORM (Kantonian or Alolan) is in the Terarium. Form is unverified.
5. Ogerpon: Serebii confirms only the Teal Mask form (Dreaded Den). Other masks: no catch method found.
6. Oricorio: Serebii lists Baile and Pom-Pom wild in Paldea, no wild Pa'u or Sensu. Nectar change is stated. Nectar availability in base game is not stated.

## Conflicts and unverified items

- Serebii's Kitakami and Blueberry dex pages list names and types only. They give no form names and no obtain method. Form claims from them rest on image filenames. Do not treat them as proof.
- The "Blueberry Terarium" claim for Alolan/Galarian/Hisuian forms is not stated on any page read. Confirm on Bulbapedia or in game.
- Vulpix/Sandshrew version exclusivity matches the owner's claim for the Blueberry dex entries (Scarlet has the Vulpix line, Violet has the Sandshrew line; each trades to the other). The form is not stated.
- Johto Wooper: both routes seem true (Cascarrafa NPC trade; wild in Kitakami). The Kitakami claim comes from search summaries. Verify.
- Kanto Tauros: Kitakami breeding is stated by Serebii. A Blueberry Savanna fixed encounter exists but its breed is unnamed. The owner's "Kanto Tauros in Blueberry" is unverified.
- Ogerpon: only Teal Mask is confirmed. Other masks are unverified, not confirmed unobtainable.
- Vivillon: the postcard rule is on one page. The region-to-pattern map is not given.
- Oricorio: only Baile and Pom-Pom are wild in Paldea per Serebii. Nectar availability in base game is unverified.
- Not fetched: Floette/Florges, Poltchageist/Sinistcha, most regional-form species, Tatsugiri, Maushold, Dudunsparce, Toxtricity, Lycanroc, Shellos/Gastrodon, gender-form species.
- Bulbapedia was not used. No row has two-source confirmation, so "single-source" rows are weaker than "confirmed".
